FAQ

Questions? We've got reps of answers.

Do I need any equipment?

Nope. Tell bAI buddies you have nothing and it'll build a bodyweight plan. Add gear anytime and the plan adapts.

Do I have to use my voice?

Not at all. Every conversation works by typing too. Voice is there when your hands are busy.

What's the difference between FitBuddy and MealBuddy?

FitBuddy handles training: workouts, splits and progressions. MealBuddy handles nutrition: macros, recipes and meal prep. Switch between them anytime from the top nav.

Is this medical advice?

No. bAI buddies is a general fitness and nutrition assistant. Check with a professional before starting a new program, especially if you have an injury or health condition.
